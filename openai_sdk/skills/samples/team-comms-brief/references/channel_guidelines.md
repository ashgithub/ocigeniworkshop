# Channel Guidelines

## Slack

- Lead with status and impact in the first two lines.
- Use short sections: `What changed`, `Why it matters`, `Try it`, `Ask`.
- Include one clear owner or channel for questions.
- Avoid long background. Link to docs or handoff notes when available.
- Do not include secrets, private customer names, credentials, or unreleased dates.

## Outlook

- Use a subject that names the artifact and the requested action.
- Include a short context paragraph for people who missed Slack.
- Add concrete next steps and a deadline when one is known.
- Include an owner, review path, and escalation path.
- Keep the email skimmable. Teams often forward it.

## Innovation Workshop Tone

- Prefer enablement language: "try", "review", "reuse", "adapt".
- Explain constraints without sounding blocked.
- Make examples easy for other teams to copy into their own work.
